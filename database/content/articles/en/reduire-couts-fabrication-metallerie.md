## Why is metalwork particularly affected?

Metalwork – the fabrication of steel structures, stairs, railings, doors and windows, cladding – is a sector where labour accounts for a large share of the cost price. Cutting, welding, finishing, assembly: every step requires skilled staff. With labour market tensions in Western Europe, these costs have risen sharply.

## Lever 1: partial or full outsourcing

Outsourcing all or part of production to a partner in Eastern Europe gives you a significant cost advantage without leaving the EU regulatory zone. Parts or assemblies are made to your drawings and specifications in certified workshops, then delivered ready to install.

## Lever 2: standardising drawings

Well-documented drawings with clearly defined tolerances reduce technical back-and-forth and non-conformities. A complete technical file sent to the subcontractor from the start optimises production times and minimises costly corrections.

## Lever 3: grouping orders

The fixed costs of starting production (machine programming, set-up, logistics) are spread over the volume. Grouping several projects into one order, or anticipating recurring needs to order in series, significantly reduces the unit cost.

## Lever 4: choosing the right partner

A reliable subcontractor with suitable machinery and proven experience in your type of production will deliver less scrap, meet deadlines better and require fewer corrective checks. Careful partner selection is itself a long-term cost-saving lever.
