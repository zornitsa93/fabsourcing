## A concrete answer to cost pressure

For several years, Western European manufacturers have faced a difficult equation: maintaining production quality while controlling costs that keep rising. Wages, social charges, energy, raw materials – every item weighs more heavily on margins. Outsourcing to Eastern Europe has emerged as a structural answer to this pressure.

## Real savings, with no compromise on quality

In Bulgaria, the cost of skilled labour in metalworking is 40–60% lower than in France or Germany. This difference translates directly into the cost price of metal parts and assemblies. Yet these savings do not come with lower quality: partner workshops work to the same European requirements (CE compliance, traceability, quality) as their Western counterparts.

## Geographical proximity, a decisive advantage

Unlike Asian subcontracting, Eastern Europe offers valuable geographical proximity. Transport times to Western Europe are counted in days, not weeks. This accessibility makes workshop visits, on-site quality checks and quick adjustments during production much easier.

## A shared regulatory framework

EU member states share the same legal and regulatory framework: CE compliance, intellectual property protection, harmonised labour law. This consistency reduces contractual risk and simplifies the administration of international orders.

## Local support makes the difference

The success of outsourcing largely depends on the quality of the intermediary.
